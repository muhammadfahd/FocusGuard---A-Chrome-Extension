# **FocusGuard Privacy Policy**

**Last Updated:** February 15, 2026

## Overview
FocusGuard ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle data when you use our Chrome extension.

## Data Collection
FocusGuard collects and stores the following data **locally on your device only**:

### What We Collect:
- **Website URLs**: Domain names of websites you visit (e.g., "youtube.com")
- **Time Data**: Duration spent on each website
- **User Preferences**: Your custom time limits and settings
- **Category Data**: Automatic categorization of websites (social media, educational, etc.)

### What We DO NOT Collect:
- Personal information (name, email, address)
- Passwords or login credentials
- Page content or form data
- Full browsing history (only domain names)
- Location data
- Financial information
- Any data from incognito/private browsing

## How We Use Data
All data is used exclusively for:
- Displaying your browsing time statistics
- Enforcing time limits you set
- Showing productivity insights
- Providing Pomodoro timer functionality

## Data Storage
- **100% Local Storage**: All data is stored using Chrome's local storage API on your device
- **No Cloud Sync**: We do not upload any data to external servers
- **No Third Parties**: We never share, sell, or transfer your data to anyone
- **User Control**: You can clear all data at any time through the extension settings

## Data Retention
- Daily statistics are automatically reset at midnight
- Time limits and preferences persist until you manually delete them
- Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data

## Permissions Explanation
- **tabs**: To identify which website you're currently viewing
- **storage**: To save your settings and time data locally
- **alarms**: To schedule daily data resets at midnight
- **scripting**: To display block pages when time limits are reached
- **host permissions (<all_urls>)**: To track time across all websites you choose to monitor

## Children's Privacy
FocusGuard is designed for students aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
